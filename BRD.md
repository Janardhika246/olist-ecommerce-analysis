# Business Requirements Document: E-Commerce Sales and Delivery Analysis

## 1. Business problem
The company wants to understand its sales performance and find what
affects customer satisfaction.

## 2. Goals
- Analyse revenue, orders and product performance
- Measure cancellations and delivery performance
- Identify what drives low customer review scores

## 3. User stories
- As a manager, I want to see monthly revenue so that I can plan stock.
- As an operations lead, I want to see late deliveries so that I can
  improve delivery performance.

## 4. Findings
- The dataset has 98,666 orders with items and about 13.59 million in total
  revenue, an average of about 137.76 per order.
- Monthly revenue peaked at 1.01 million in November 2017.
- Top categories: health and beauty, watches and gifts, bed/bath/table.
- Cancellation rate is 0.63%, which is low.
- Average delivery time is 12.5 days.
- 7,701 of 96,358 delivered orders (about 8%) arrived late.
- Late orders scored 2.57 on average, compared with 4.29 for on-time orders.
- Credit card accounts for the largest share of payment value.

## 5. Recommendations
1. Prioritise delivery performance, because cancellations are low (0.63%).
2. Monitor late deliveries by seller and region.
3. Set more realistic delivery estimates.

*Note: late delivery and low reviews are associated. This analysis does
not prove lateness is the only cause.*
